Candidate: mac/.build/SignedDevelopment/Build/Products/Debug/NostrWriter.app
Build: 2026-09-18 22:22 +0100.
Driver: System Events keystroke from an escalated osascript that also sets the
process frontmost. Two capability facts were established first:

  - The computer-use runtime's own modifier handling does NOT reach the app:
    sky.press_key with modifiers ["cmd"], ["command"], ["meta"] or ["Cmd"] each
    delivered the bare letter key instead (three probes typed literal "f"
    characters into the fixture at the caret). That accidental edit was undone
    through the app's own Edit > Undo Typing and the fixture text was restored
    to "> A quoted passage attributed to nobody." before any evidence was taken.
  - System Events keystroke WITH modifiers DOES reach the app. Validated with
    Cmd+N, which created a new document ("Untitled 3"; the tab bar went from
    three to four tabs).

Observations on the running candidate, all driven as real key events:

  1. Cmd+N            -> a new document/tab appeared. (modifier delivery probe)
  2. Cmd+Z            -> undid typed characters; the document returned to empty
                         and the status returned to "0 words - Unsaved".
                         (Also used to repair the accidental "f" probes.)
  3. Cmd+Shift+F      -> View > Focus Writing engaged: the accessibility tree
                         lost "Document sidebar" and the toolbar entirely, the
                         splitter moved to 0, and the editor became full width
                         while the status line ("0 words - Unsaved"), the
                         "Retry Recovery / Recording off" banner and the tab bar
                         remained. A second Cmd+Shift+F restored the sidebar and
                         toolbar.
  4. Ctrl+Cmd+F       -> View > Enter Full Screen engaged: the window's close and
                         minimize buttons disappeared from the accessibility
                         tree (native fullscreen hides the traffic lights). A
                         second Ctrl+Cmd+F restored both buttons.

Not exercised in this session:
  - Reduce Motion. NSWorkspace.shared.accessibilityDisplayShouldReduceMotion is
    read by the app (WriterWindowController.swift:293), and the host currently
    has no reduceMotion key in com.apple.universalaccess, i.e. the setting is
    off. Its visible effect is an animation change that a single-frame capture
    cannot show, so it needs an owner session with the setting toggled.
  - Typewriter Scrolling as an interaction. View > Typewriter Scrolling exists
    (selector toggleTypewriterScrolling:) and was toggled on and then off from
    the View menu without error, but its effect is a viewport shift during
    typing that the accessibility tree does not expose.
